# Setting and Background
- You are a space pirate raiding industrial autonomous facilities in distant planets
- Civilization has expanded to inhabit entire systems
- Faster than light travel does not exist yet, so colonization is done autonomously and people are few and far between
- In contested regions of space, these facilities manufacture robots and other weapons. they are operated fully autonomously and have internal defense but will call for organic reinforcements, which take a while to arrive
	- facilities do not have human combat supplies such as medicine or guns stored as the defenses well aren't people and such equipment is useless
## [Printers](Crafting.md)
- In the game/story, printers exist for manual maintenance of the facility by its creators. The player prints attachments and other useful gear by looking for them in the print history
- 3d printers in the facility are encrypted, so it is not possible to bring your own little flash drive of items you want to print

# [(fox character) Active Camouflage Suit](Classes.md)
- External elements provide camouflage from thermals, radar, and regular vision
- To do this, the suit must match the outside temperature, which cools the exterior but also heats the interior
- Combined with having to mask the heat generated from gunfire, the internal temperature can cook the user alive if kept on
