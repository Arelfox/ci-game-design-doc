# 1. Player Vehicle
(name tbd)
The player will enter the facility via an entrance vehicle. this is the centerpiece of a run, providing cover, [ammunition](Ammo.md), resource storage, and transportation throughout the facility.
Certain objects, such as armor plates or ammo modules, can be welded freely on the vehicle.
The player is responsible for their creations and has absolute freedom to be creative with their inventions, as this is the intention of the game.
The player vehicle should not be used for combat. At most, low caliber automated turrets with low health pools can be placed.
Players cannot fire through windows, as it would make it too easy to hide inside the car
The run ends if the vehicle is destroyed, as it is required for extraction
