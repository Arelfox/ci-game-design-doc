# 1. Health
- There is no natural healing.
- The health system will be separated by limbs, and damaging each part will have different consequences:
	- Head
		- Greatly increase pain
	- Arms
		- increase weapon sway, decrease handling
	- Legs
		- decrease walk speed
		- disable sprinting if too damaged
- Certain [items](Items.md) can recover lost limb health, reduce injury effects, and prevent damage
- Most forms of damage will cause bleeding, which slowly causes additional damage if not stopped
- Due to scope limitations, limbs will not explode when their health is 0 :(
- Players will die if their overall "blood health" is zero (influenced by global bleeding), or a vital part's health is zero (torso and head)
# 2. Pain
Damage and certain healing items will cause pain.
Pain temporarily increases limb damage effects and blurs the screen

# 3. Healing
- Medical [items](Items.md) are used with LMB to quick heal (automatically chooses limb with worst condition) or RMB to manually select a limb
- UI of the player's condition on the bottom left, no dedicated health panel UI
