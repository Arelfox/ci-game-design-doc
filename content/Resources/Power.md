# Power
- Power is a valuable resource which allows certain [medical items](Items.md), shields, [class abilities](Classes.md), and other equipment to operate.
- Power cells are found in the map, from salvaging destroyed enemies, and rare [printing](Crafting.md) recipes. They can be placed into equipment to provide power
- Once depleted, they can either be recharged with special [abilities](Classes.md) or scrapped for some metal.
- When power cells are stacked in the [inventory](Inventory.md) and unstacked, they will divide their power evenly
