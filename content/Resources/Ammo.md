# 1. Ammo
Ammo is created through a modular "crafting" system. The [vehicle](Vehicle.md)'s ammo manufacturing machine creates the basic ammo, which is then routed through upgrade modules that apply certain effects to the round. These modules are obtained via [printing](Crafting.md). Ammo must be routed last into a mag loading machine, which takes player magazines and fills them over time with the specified caliber round, with the effects applied before.
## 1.2 Ammo Calibers
- 5.56×45 (light)
	- no apds/airburst option
	- less explosive aphe, he
- 6.8×51 (medium)
- 7.62×51 (older medium)
- 8.6×60 (heavy)
- 12.7×99 (anti material)
- 8.5x60 ET (electrothermal cartridge)
- 9x19 (light pistol)
	- no apds/airbust option
- 10x25 (medium pistol)
	- no apds/airburst option
- 12.7x33 (heavy pistol)
- 10 gauge (medium shotgun)
- 12 gauge (light shotgun)
Ammo calibers exist for immersion purposes. Since ammo is effectively unlimited, it is almost entirely cosmetic.
# 2. Gun Magazines
- Mags must be loaded in the magazine loader machine, placed on vehicles
- The amount left in your inventory compatible with the equipped gun will appear on the ui
- While ammo is effectively unlimited, magazines are not. The amount and type of magazines you bring will determine your combat effectiveness, and therefore is worth printing better ones.
## 2.1 Reloading
- tap r
	- reloads and picks the most full (tiebroken by rightmost in inventory for fine tuning) mag
- double tap r
	- same thing but drops the mag on ground for a quicker reload (can be picked up ofc)
- hold r
	- opens ui wheel to select which mag to load
